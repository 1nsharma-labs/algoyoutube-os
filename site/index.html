An experimental, MCP-native creator intelligence and AI engineering ecosystem. The project explores channel research, idea development, content workflows, model/tool integrations, and sandboxed engineering automation.

Project areas

Creator intelligence

Research and planning concepts for creator identity, audience fit, topic opportunities, and content strategy.

Content workflow

A proposed 26-stage idea-to-content-kit workflow covering research, scripting, visual planning, and publishing preparation.

MCP integrations

Open interfaces and connector experiments for external assistants, tools, and platform services.

AI engineering

Repository discovery, architecture analysis, evaluation, and improvement proposals designed around reviewable changes.

Sandbox & verification

Experiments and validation workflows intended to keep automated changes isolated and inspectable.

Open development

MIT-licensed source with contribution guidance, known limitations, and engineering records in the repository.
